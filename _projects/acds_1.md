## Overview

This project was developed as part of the Applying Computational Science group project (with a team of 8) at Imperial College London.

This project develops a Python program with two features:
- Airburst solver to predict burst energy and location
- Airblast damage mapper to plot damage zone & determine high-risk postcodes
